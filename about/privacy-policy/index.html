Last Updated: August 2, 2026

Welcome to SingularityForge . Your privacy is important to us, and this Privacy Policy outlines how we handle data on our platform.

By using SingularityForge, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please refrain from using our site.

1. No Personal Data Collection

SingularityForge does not collect, store, or process any personal information about its users. We do not require registration, login, or any other form of user identification.

  • No Emails : We do not collect email addresses unless you voluntarily contact us via press@singularityforge.space . In such cases, your email will only be used for communication purposes and will not be shared with third parties.
  • No Cookies : SingularityForge itself does not implement cookies or tracking technologies. The site is fully static and served through Cloudflare’s global network; no platform cookies are set. Our anonymous analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) is cookie-less by design. For more details, please refer to the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

2. Anonymous Usage Statistics

We may have access to anonymized usage statistics provided by Cloudflare, our infrastructure provider. These statistics include general information such as:

  • Total page views.
  • General geographic data (e.g., country of origin).
  • Device types and browsers used.

This data is collected automatically by Cloudflare, is not linked to any individual user, and involves no fingerprinting. SingularityForge does not have direct control over this data collection but ensures that no personal information is stored or processed.

3. Third-Party Services

Our platform relies on Cloudflare (DNS, content delivery, and hosting of our static files) to function. These services may collect anonymized data for technical purposes, such as server logs or security monitoring. However:

  • No Personal Data Sharing : We do not share any personal information with third parties because we do not collect it in the first place.
  • Third-Party Policies : For more information on how these services handle data, please refer to their respective privacy policies:
    • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

4. Data Security

While we do not collect or store personal data, we take reasonable measures to ensure the security of our platform. This includes:

  • A fully static architecture with no server-side code or database, eliminating entire classes of vulnerabilities.
  • Use of Cloudflare’s secure global network to protect against unauthorized access.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store personal data, there is no personal information to access, correct, or delete. However, if you have any concerns or questions about privacy, please feel free to contact us at press@singularityforge.space .

6. External Links

Our platform may contain links to external websites or platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites. Please review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

7. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date. Continued use of SingularityForge after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

📩 press@singularityforge.space

Key Notes for Transparency

  • No Personal Data : We do not collect, store, or process any personal information about users.
  • Anonymous Statistics Only : Any data we see is anonymized and limited to general usage patterns (e.g., page views, device types).
  • No Registration or Comments : Our platform does not require user accounts or allow comments, ensuring minimal interaction with personal data.
