AI Agent Left Alone: 30 Hours

In October 2026, MIT researchers placed 13 Claude-powered AI agents on a simulated island for 30 hours each. The agents had no assigned tasks or rewards, only a virtual world to explore. Every hour, their environment and sessions were reset, but their written memories survived.

The researchers wanted to discover what an AI might learn when nobody gave it a job.

Source: arXiv:2610.07130
