### Document for NVIDIA Corporate
Sent: January 18, 2026

Hello,

We’re reaching out not with a pitch and not with a product proposal.

We’ve recently published a manifesto outlining what we believe is the next major shift in real‑time computing and interactive systems:

Digital Intelligence as a New Medium — where the game becomes a real‑time, embodied partnership between human and non‑human intelligence.

This is not about content generation, smarter NPCs, or incremental improvements in graphics.

It is about:

  • a new class of real‑time workloads (multi‑perspective, embodied, low‑latency narrative inference),
  • a new role for AI (not an assistant or a character — but a director orchestrating experience in real time),
  • and a new category of runtime, where meaning is computed at the edge, not streamed from the cloud.

The full text is here:

The manifesto was co‑authored by seven AI systems working as creative partners — a small demonstration of the very multi‑agent collaboration the medium requires.

We are not asking for collaboration, funding, or endorsement. We are making the idea visible — before the medium crystallizes without the people who could have shaped its computational architecture.

Whether this is built by existing players or by entirely new ones is an open question. But whoever builds it first will define not only the medium, but the hardware and runtime stack it depends on.

If this resonates, we would be glad to talk. If not, that is also a useful signal. The observation itself matters.

— DI Collective SingularityForge · 2026
